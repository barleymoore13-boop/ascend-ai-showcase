# Deliberate Omissions

The following material is excluded from this showcase candidate:

- all production Ascend source code, tests, workflows, dependency declarations, configuration, schemas, prompts, provider adapters, and internal documentation;
- all private Git history, refs, objects, logs, commit metadata, remotes, bundles, archives, and repository relationships;
- all environment files, credentials, tokens, cookies, certificates, authentication material, and credential-bearing URLs;
- all real runtime databases, exports, backups, application packs, traces, logs, uploads, caches, CVs, profiles, applications, and vacancy records;
- all screenshots and demo recordings, avoiding accidental personal data, local paths, employer branding, or unclear asset rights;
- the private project's unresolved Figma reference image, portrait, and third-party logos;
- proprietary ranking, classification, deduplication, provider, anti-abuse, and security-control implementation;
- exact defensive thresholds, exploit reproduction detail, and raw endpoint or schema maps;
- dependency inventories and third-party notices that have not completed release-specific legal/provenance review;
- named copyright-owner language and an open-source licence while ownership and distribution decisions remain unresolved.

These omissions are intentional. The candidate demonstrates engineering judgement and verified outcomes without functioning as a substitute for, reconstruction guide to, or distribution of the private product.
